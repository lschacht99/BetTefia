History from the 360 archive

A grand synagogue overlooking the Hudson.

Beth Tephilah means “House of Prayer.” The congregation’s roots go back to 1850, and the River Street building was constructed in 1909 when Troy’s Jewish community was large enough to build on a grand scale overlooking the nearby Hudson River.

The exterior combines a towering entry section with a fine copper dome and a horizontal sanctuary section. Inside, the drama is the long barrel-vaulted sanctuary, Doric columns, and women’s balcony.
