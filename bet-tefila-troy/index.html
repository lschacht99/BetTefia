History has to be visible

A landmark with a pulse.

Beth Tephilah is located in Troy’s Central Historic District and was placed on the National Register of Historic Places in May 2016. The message is simple: this is protected American Jewish history, and it is still alive.

The building

A grand synagogue overlooking the Hudson.

The River Street building was constructed in 1909 when Troy’s Jewish community was large enough to build on a grand scale overlooking the nearby Hudson River.

The exterior combines a towering entry section with a fine copper dome and a horizontal sanctuary section. Inside, the drama is the long barrel-vaulted sanctuary, Doric columns, and women’s balcony.
