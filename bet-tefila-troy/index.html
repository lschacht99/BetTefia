About

Warm, historic, and communal.

Beth Tephila is located in Troy’s Central Historic District and was placed on the National Register of Historic Places in May 2016. The message is simple: this is protected American Jewish history, and it remains a living home for faith, family, and community.

Architecture & space

A synagogue that takes your breath away.

The building

Neo-Classical. Doric columns. Copper dome.

The 1909 building was designed for a larger Jewish Troy — with a monumental portico, soaring copper dome, long barrel-vaulted sanctuary, women's balcony, and Hudson River views. It was built to last centuries. And it has.

  • Neo-Classical exterior with Doric columns
  • Towering entry section and copper dome
  • Long barrel-vaulted sanctuary ceiling
  • Women's balcony supported by columns
  • Located in Troy's Central Historic District

History alive

On the National Register since 2016.

Samuel Gruber called it "a grand historic synagogue overlooking the Hudson." Synagogues360, the ANU Museum of the Jewish People's digital archive, chose Beth Tephilah for its 360° virtual tour collection.

Rabbi Laibel Morrison has led this congregation since 1982. The building and community are inseparable — neither is a museum piece.
