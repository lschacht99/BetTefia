Bring your simcha into a living historic synagogue.

Beth Tephila is not “an event hall.” It is a sacred house of prayer that can welcome Jewish milestones with dignity: engagements, Bar and Bat Mitzvahs, Brit Milah, Upsherin/Chalakah first haircuts, Shabbat meals, holiday tables, and small family simchos.
